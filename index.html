Privacy Policy Effective Date: 20-10-2026 Emoji Memory Quest (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information. 1. Information Collection and Use Emoji Memory Quest is a memory puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts. We do not sell your information. Most gameplay data, such as game progress, settings, unlocked levels, selected themes, sound settings, and other preferences, is stored locally on your device. We use Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand how the app is used, so we can improve it. See Section 2. If you choose to use Google Play Games Services features such as leaderboards, Google may process limited game-related information through your Google account. See Section 4. 2. Analytics (Firebase Analytics) The app uses Firebase Analytics. It collects: - App interactions, such as screens viewed, levels completed, hints used, and whether the upgrade screen was shown, closed, or started. - Device and app identifiers, such as a Firebase app instance ID, and general device and app information such as device model, operating system version, and app version. This data is used only for analytics, to understand how players use the app and to improve it. It is not used for advertising. The app does not use the Android advertising ID. We do not share this data with third parties for their own purposes and we do not sell it. We do not link it to your name or identity. Firebase is operated by Google and processes this data under Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy 3. Permissions Emoji Memory Quest does not request or use any sensitive device permissions. 4. Google Play Games Services Emoji Memory Quest uses Google Play Games Services to provide leaderboard functionality. Sign-in starts only when you choose to show your score on the leaderboard. When you sign in to Google Play Games, Google may collect or process information associated with your Google Play Games profile, such as your player name, player ID, profile image, scores, rankings, and related gameplay activity. This information is used only to display leaderboard scores and rankings. You can manage your Google Play Games profile, sign-in, and privacy settings through your Google account and Google Play Games settings. 5. In-App Purchases Emoji Memory Quest offers optional in-app purchases, including a one-time Pro upgrade. All purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, receive, or store your payment details, such as credit card number, debit card number, UPI details, bank account details, or other financial information. Google Play may provide us limited purchase-related information, such as product ID, purchase status, purchase token, order status, and purchase time, only for verifying and delivering the purchased item or benefit. All payments, refunds, and purchase processing are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google Play’s payment and refund policies. 6. Third-Party Services Emoji Memory Quest uses the following third-party services: - Firebase Analytics, for app usage analytics - Google Play Games Services, for leaderboard functionality - Google Play Billing, for optional in-app purchases - Google Play Services, for supporting Google features in the app These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. 7. Data Storage and Deletion Game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this local data. We do not operate our own server to store personal user data. Analytics data is held by Firebase (Google). To ask us about or request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, contact us at the email address in Section 11. 8. Children’s Privacy Emoji Memory Quest is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help with removal of any related data. 9. Data Security Data sent to Firebase Analytics is encrypted in transit. Data processed through Google Play Games Services or Google Play Billing is handled by Google using its own security practices. 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. 11.
