Privacy Policy Effective Date: 28-04-2026 Emoji Memory Quest (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information. 1. Information Collection and Use Emoji Memory Quest is designed to be a fun and safe memory puzzle game. We do not directly collect, store, sell, or share any personally identifiable information from users. Most gameplay data, such as game progress, settings, unlocked levels, selected themes, sound settings, and other preferences, may be stored locally on your device. If you choose to use Google Play Games Services features such as leaderboards, Google may process limited game-related information through your Google account, such as your Play Games profile, player ID, leaderboard score, and gameplay activity required for leaderboard functionality. We use this information only to provide leaderboard features and improve the gameplay experience. 2. Permissions Emoji Memory Quest does not request or use any sensitive device permissions. 3. Google Play Games Services Emoji Memory Quest uses Google Play Games Services to provide leaderboard functionality. When you sign in to Google Play Games, Google may collect or process information associated with your Google Play Games profile, such as your player name, player ID, profile image, scores, rankings, and related gameplay activity. This information is used only to display leaderboard scores and rankings inside Google Play Games features. You can manage your Google Play Games profile, sign-in, and privacy settings through your Google account and Google Play Games settings. 4. In-App Purchases Emoji Memory Quest offers optional in-app purchases, such as “Support the Developer.” All purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, receive, or store your payment details, such as credit card number, debit card number, UPI details, bank account details, or other financial information. Google Play may provide us limited purchase-related information, such as product ID, purchase status, purchase token, order status, and purchase time, only for verifying and delivering the purchased item or benefit. All payments, refunds, and purchase processing are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google Play’s payment and refund policies. 5. Third-Party Services Emoji Memory Quest may use the following third-party services: - Google Play Games Services, for leaderboard functionality - Google Play Billing, for optional in-app purchases - Google Play Services, for supporting Google features in the app These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or analytics tools that collect user data. 6. Data Storage Game progress, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Leaderboard-related data may be handled by Google Play Games Services if you choose to sign in and use leaderboard features. Purchase-related verification data may be handled by Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases. We do not operate our own server to collect or store personal user data. 7. Children’s Privacy Emoji Memory Quest is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any Google Play Games or purchase-related data is handled by Google through Google Play Services and Google Play Billing, subject to Google’s policies and parental controls. Parents or guardians can manage purchases, Google Play Games access, and privacy settings through Google Play and Google account settings. 8. Data Security We do not directly collect or store personal information on our own servers. Any data processed through Google Play Games Services or Google Play Billing is handled by Google using its own security practices. 9.
