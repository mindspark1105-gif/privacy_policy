Daynora privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Daynora is an Android habit and personal-record app. This policy explains what it stores and what it sends.

1. No Daynora account is required

You can use the app's local features without creating a Daynora account. Optional Google Drive features require you to connect a Google account and grant Google Drive permission. Daynora stores the selected Google account email address on your phone to identify that connection. There is no Daynora-hosted account or developer-operated backup server.

2. Data stored on your phone

Daynora stores the information you enter, including habits, check-ins, streaks, reflections, trackers, calorie entries, profile details, family profiles, health documents and reminder settings, on your device. Buddy names and phone numbers that you enter are also stored locally. These entries are not sent to Firebase Analytics. They can leave your phone if you choose Google Drive backup, record upload, export or sharing as explained below. Only add another person's information when you have their permission.

3. Optional Google Drive record storage and full backup

Google Drive connection is optional. If you allow record storage, selected record files are uploaded to folders in your own Google Drive. If you enable full backup, the app can also upload snapshots automatically after changes. A full backup can include habits and check-ins, reflections, trackers, calorie and profile information, family profiles, health records and their files, reminder settings and app preferences.

These files are sent directly to Google Drive under the Google account you selected. Daynora requests the drive.file permission to work with files it creates or you authorize. The developer does not receive a copy on a separate server. Google handles this data under its policies and your Google account controls.

Pausing backup stops future scheduled backup jobs. It does not erase copies already in Drive. You can manage or delete those files in Google Drive and revoke the app's access through your Google account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, but does not by itself remove Drive copies.

Daynora does not add its own password encryption to exported backups or Drive snapshots. Google Drive and your Google account supply their own storage and access protections. Do not share these files with people you do not want to read them.

4. Health-related information

Profile measurements, calorie entries, family records and medicine/reminder information support the features you choose. They are not sent to Firebase Analytics. They remain on your device unless you use the optional backup, upload, export or sharing features. Calorie targets are estimates, not medical advice. Daynora is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Seek a healthcare professional's advice for medical questions.

5. Firebase Analytics

Daynora uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app use. The app's custom events have fixed names, such as onboarding completed, Pro screen opened, record saved or Drive connected. These custom events do not include the habit names, notes, health information, file contents or other text you type.

The Analytics SDK also processes app-instance identifiers, app/device information, app sessions and interaction events, approximate location derived from IP addresses, and purchase/subscription event information where collected by the SDK. Daynora disables advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization signals. Analytics is on by default in the app and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy. Turning it off stops new Analytics collection; it does not automatically delete data already processed by Google.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Purchases

Optional Pro subscriptions are handled by Google Play. The app checks purchases and subscription status to provide Pro access. The developer does not receive your card or bank details. Google's purchase and subscription terms apply.

7. Reminders and alarms

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Optional alarm features use Android permissions you grant and can revoke in system settings. Reminder delivery can depend on Android notification, alarm and battery settings.

8. Export and sharing

Exports and shared reports are created when you choose those actions. Buddy messages open in your messaging app for you to review and send; Daynora does not send them automatically. Files and messages you send are also handled by their destination service's policies. Exported files are not encrypted by Daynora, so store and share them carefully.

9. No sale of data

We do not sell your data or use your habit or health entries for advertising.

10. Children

Daynora is not directed at children under 13.
