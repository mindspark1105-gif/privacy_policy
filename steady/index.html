Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what stays on your phone and what may leave it. It covers the habit-only Daynora 2.0.0 build 34 and explains how data from older versions is treated.

No Daynora account required

You can use local habits without a Daynora account. Google Drive backup is optional and asks you to connect your own Google account. Daynora stores the connected account's email on your phone to identify that connection. It does not receive your Google password.

Habit data on your phone

Your habits, editable targets, check-in dates, streaks, notes, reminder times and app settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Optional buddy names and phone numbers that you enter are stored with your habit data. Daynora does not read your address book. Widgets and reminders use this same local habit data.

The habit-only version has no medical-record import or viewer, medicine reminders, calorie or meal tracking, body measurements, daily mood or energy reflections, or separate numeric trackers. It does not access Health Connect or health sensors. Its starter ideas are editable routine checklists, not health advice.

Optional Google Drive backup and restore

If you connect Google and explicitly enable backup, Daynora sends your habit data and supported app settings to your own Google Drive. This includes habit names, notes, targets, check-ins, reminder settings and optional buddy contact details. Daynora uses Google Drive's drive.file access for files created or opened through the app, rather than unrestricted access to all your Drive files.

Backups use your Google account's storage. They are not instant sync. Android schedules work in the background and may delay it. Backups are not end-to-end encrypted by Daynora. Google processes the files under its own terms and privacy policy. Daynora does not send these backups to a Daynora-operated server.

You can turn off automatic backup or disconnect Google in the app. Existing copies remain in Drive until you delete them there. Restore requires the same Google account and your confirmation before replacing supported local data. A local recovery copy is saved before replacement. Purchases and sign-in credentials are not restored from backup.

Local exports, share cards and buddy messages

When you choose Export or Share, data goes to the destination you select. Local exports contain habits and their saved details. Habit and report cards can show habit names, targets, check-ins and streak history. Review the preview and the recipient before sharing. Export files are not encrypted by Daynora; anyone you give them to can read their contents.

Buddy names and phone numbers you enter may be included in optional backups. For messages, Daynora opens your messaging app with prepared text and you choose whether to send it. The messaging app or carrier handles sent messages under its own policies.

Usage analytics

Google Firebase Analytics is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy. Turning it off stops future collection through that setting; it does not automatically delete information already sent to Google.

Daynora sends fixed event names about feature use, such as completing onboarding, creating your first habit, viewing Pro or Stats, opening or sharing a report, exporting a backup, or connecting Drive. These event calls do not include the habit names, notes, targets, buddy details or other text you type.

Firebase also receives automatic app and device information, an app-instance identifier, app lifecycle and interaction events, approximate location derived from masked IP addresses, and in-app purchase or subscription events such as product and price information. This is not a promise that no data leaves your phone or that analytics is fully anonymous. Daynora does not use the Android advertising ID, sell this data or use it for advertising. Google processes information under https://policies.google.com/privacy. Firebase's disclosure is available at https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11582702.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases, free-trial eligibility, payments and subscription processing are handled by Google Play. Daynora receives purchase and entitlement information to check whether Pro is active and caches an entitlement result on your phone. Daynora does not receive your card or bank details. Prices, trial eligibility, renewal and cancellation terms are shown by Google Play. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally. Optional alarm-style ringing uses Android's Alarms and reminders access, which you control. Notification and battery settings can affect delivery; exact delivery is not guaranteed.

Data from older versions

Upgrading does not silently delete older medical documents, family profiles, meal entries, body profiles, reflections or custom-tracker data already stored on your phone by earlier versions. Build 34 does not display, upload, export, restore or schedule this legacy data. Old medicine alarms are stopped. Prior full-app automatic-backup consent is paused, and habit-only backup requires a new opt-in.

Older full-app backup files and document copies already in your Google Drive remain there until you delete them. The habit-only version restores its new habit-only cloud-backup format, not older full-app cloud snapshots. Older local exports can import habits only; removed features are not restored.

If you are still using an older full-feature version, its optional record-copy and full-backup features can include family profiles, health records, document files, meals, reflections, trackers and associated details in your own Google Drive after your consent. Those older versions may include bundled on-device ML Kit text recognition. The habit-only build removes ML Kit and OCR entirely. Update to build 34 to use the habit-only feature set.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Save any backup you want to keep before clearing storage. Clearing local storage or uninstalling does not cancel Google Play subscriptions or delete Drive copies. Delete those copies in your own Google Drive and manage subscriptions separately in Google Play.

Optional backup files remain until you delete them in Drive. Usage information already sent to Google is subject to the configured analytics retention and Google's service policies. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to mindspark1105@gmail.com. We will explain what we can delete and what you control in your own Google account; we cannot delete files in a disconnected Google account for you.

Network calls to Google services use HTTPS. This does not mean exported files or local app storage are end-to-end encrypted.

Daynora is not directed at children under 13.
